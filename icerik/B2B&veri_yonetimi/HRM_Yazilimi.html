What is HRM Software?

As the number of employees grows in companies, manually tracking personnel information, leaves, salaries, and recruitment processes becomes quite difficult. HRM (Human Resource Management) software combines these processes required by the human resources department into a single system.

For example, the operations that take place when a new employee starts work are managed as follows:

  • Personnel information is saved in the system.
  • The employee's role and department are defined.
  • Leave details are established.
  • Salary and fringe benefits are tracked.
  • Performance evaluations can be processed into the system.

Thus, the human resources department can manage all operations related to employees from a single center, efficiently and error-free.

Key Features of HRM Software

1. Personnel Management

Employees' personal and corporate information can be stored centrally. The following data can be easily tracked through the system:

  • Department and position information
  • Hire date and employment status
  • Contact information and contract details

2. Leave and Absence Tracking

Employees can digitally submit leave requests through the system. Managers can instantly review these requests and approve or reject them. All leave history is recorded automatically.

3. Recruitment Management

Open positions can be created, and candidates' application processes can be tracked step-by-step. Candidates go through the following stages:

Application → Interview → Evaluation → Offer → Hiring

4. Performance Management

Employees' progress toward established goals is monitored. Managers can conduct periodic performance evaluations with objective criteria through the system.

5. Salary and Payroll Management

Employee salaries, bonuses, and other supplementary payments are tracked via the system. Advanced HRM solutions can also automate payroll processes.

Advantages and Disadvantages

Advantages

  • Reduces workload: Automates repetitive routine HR tasks.
  • Organizes employee info: Prevents document clutter, gathers data in a single center.
  • Simplifies leave processes: Requests are quickly approved in a digital environment.
  • Speeds up recruitment: Candidate evaluation processes are managed from a single platform.
  • Provides performance tracking: Goals and results are monitored regularly.
  • Offers reporting: Metrics like employee turnover and leave usage are reported instantly.

Disadvantages

  • Requires setup process: Migrating existing data can take time.
  • May require training: Staff training may be needed for system adaptation.
  • Can create costs: Costs rise as the number of modules and users increases.
  • Data security matters: High security is required for sensitive employee data.
  • Potential resistance: Dropping old habits can initially be difficult.
